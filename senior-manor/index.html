Our story

Built by a family who needed it

Larkspur began in 2009, when Thomas Avery searched the Valley for a place his mother would be proud to call home and could not find one. He built it instead, on a citrus grove at the foot of Camelback Mountain.

It is still family-owned. That means decisions are made here, by people who know the residents, and the standard is set by one question: would we want this for our own parents?

Thomas Avery, founderLarkspur Residences
