Life here

Every day planned around you

Residents choose how full their week is. Most mornings start with coffee on the courtyard; after that, it is up to you.

  • Classes, outings and live music on the calendar every week
  • Chef-cooked meals from a seasonal menu, with dietary needs catered for
  • A resident council that has a say in the activities and the menu
  • Family app with photos, care updates and the week’s schedule
  • Scheduled transport to appointments, church and shopping
  • Call buttons in every apartment, answered day and night

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Call (480) 555-0187.

How much does it cost?

Independent living starts at $3,200 a month and assisted living at $4,650, including meals, housekeeping and activities. Memory care is priced after a care assessment. There is no buy-in fee.

Can my parent bring their pet?

Yes. Cats and small dogs are welcome in independent and assisted living, and our team can help with walks.

What happens if care needs change?

Our nurse reviews each care plan every three months and whenever something changes. Most residents can move between levels of care without leaving the campus.

Is there a nurse on site at night?

Yes. A licensed nurse is on site 24 hours a day, and every apartment has a call button.
