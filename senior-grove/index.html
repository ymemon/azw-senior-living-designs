Questions families ask

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Call (480) 555-0163.

What does the monthly price include?

Your apartment, three meals a day, weekly housekeeping and linen, utilities except phone, scheduled transport, and every activity on the calendar.

Is there an entrance or buy-in fee?

No. There is a one-time community fee of $2,500 and then a monthly price, with no long-term contract.

Can we try it before deciding?

Yes. Short stays in a furnished apartment are available from one week, at a daily rate.

Do you accept long-term care insurance?

Yes. We help families file claims with most long-term care insurers, and can talk you through veterans’ benefits.
